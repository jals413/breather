Why Breather

Breaks that know when not to interrupt.

The 20-20-20 rule, done properly: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds — without losing your train of thought.

Lives in your notch

A heads-up grows out of the notch before each break, Dynamic Island style. Hover the notch any time for your next break.

Smart Pause

Breaks wait during calls, videos, fullscreen games, screen recording and focus apps — and until you stop typing.

Time away counts

Step away from your Mac and that time is a break. No more being told to rest right after you sat back down.

Beautiful breaks

Eight themes, animated aurora backgrounds, your own photos, five timer styles and a breathing guide.

Gentle reminders

Water, posture, blinking, standing up — counted on real screen time, with Done and Later right in the notch.

Screen Score

A daily score, screen time, your longest stretch, a seven-day view and top apps. All stored on your Mac.

Profiles

Everyday, Deep Work, Gentle — or your own. Switch from the menu bar, or automatically when you open an app.

Automations

Run Shortcuts, AppleScript or shell scripts when breaks start and end. Control it all with breather:// links.

Break screen

Make your breaks yours.

Every display fades into the break you designed — over fullscreen apps too.

Menu bar & notch

Everything at a glance.

Your next break, one tap to start, pause or skip, and your profiles — in the menu bar and in the notch. Hover the notch and it opens into a quick dashboard.

iPhone & Apple Watch

Your break, on every screen you own.

When your Mac takes a break, your iPhone shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and your Apple Watch taps your wrist. Switch profiles and see your stats from your phone.

Install

Get Breather.

Free and open source. Pick whichever you like — they all install the same app.

$brew install --cask jals413/tap/breather

Update later with brew upgrade --cask breather.

$curl -fsSL https://jals413.github.io/breather/install.sh | bash

Downloads the latest release from GitHub, checks its SHA-256, installs it to Applications and opens it. Read the script first if you like.

Download Breather.dmg

Open it and drag Breather to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS asks you to approve it — here's how (30 seconds).

$git clone https://github.com/jals413/breather.git && cd breather && scripts/build.sh --install --run

Needs Xcode 26 or later. See the README for the iPhone and Watch apps.

“Breather can't be opened because Apple cannot verify it”

Breather is free and open source, so it isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account). macOS asks you to approve it once — Homebrew and the one-line installer handle this for you. If you downloaded the DMG, follow the install guide: it takes about 30 seconds.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no analytics, no ads. Breather's only internet request is a daily check for new versions on GitHub, and you can turn it off.

Your stats stay local

Screen time and app usage are stored in your Library folder. Website stats are off unless you turn them on.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0, and every release is built publicly by GitHub Actions with published checksums.

FAQ

Questions

Is Breather really free?

Yes — free and open source under the GNU GPL v3.0. No trials, no subscriptions.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apple only “notarizes” apps from developers with a paid membership. Breather isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once. The install guide shows exactly where to click; Homebrew and the one-line installer do it for you.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac running macOS 26 Tahoe or later, including macOS 27 — Apple silicon and Intel. The notch features show as a floating pill on Macs and displays without a notch.

Will it interrupt my video calls?

No. Breather waits while any app is using your microphone or camera, while video is playing, while you're screen-recording, and until you pause typing.

How do I update?

Breather tells you in the notch when a new version is out. With Homebrew: brew upgrade --cask breather. Otherwise download the new DMG or run the one-line installer again.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Breather from the menu bar and move it to the Bin. To remove its data too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Breather. With Homebrew: brew uninstall --zap --cask breather.
